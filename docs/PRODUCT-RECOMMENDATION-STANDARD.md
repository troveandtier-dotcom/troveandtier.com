# Trove & Tier — product recommendation standard

No live products are listed in the first website release. This standard defines the evidence required before product catalogue records can be published.

## Required product record
- Stable internal product ID and editorial slug.
- Product name, brand and manufacturer model/SKU where available.
- Category and intended use.
- Market availability: US, UK, Canada separately.
- Retailer name and canonical product URL for each market.
- Affiliate destination and network/programme status, stored separately from canonical product URL.
- Dimensions with units and clear indication of assembled/product/package dimensions.
- Materials, care instructions and relevant compatibility constraints.
- Manufacturer claims linked to a source and date checked.
- Editorial summary, advantages, limitations and alternatives.
- Price only when verified, with currency, retailer and checked timestamp.
- Product image asset with usage rights / source attribution.
- Editorial evidence type: manufacturer specification, independent research, editorial assessment, hands-on testing.
- Affiliate, sponsored or gifted relationship disclosure.
- Last verified date and review status.

## Publication gates
1. No product can be published without a named source for factual specifications.
2. Never invent ratings, reviews, popularity, awards, expert endorsements or stock.
3. Never call an item tested unless documented hands-on testing actually occurred.
4. Keep regional retailer links, currency and availability distinct.
5. Do not infer a local price by currency conversion and present it as a retailer price.
6. If current price or availability is not verified, display a clear retailer-check message.
7. Affiliate links must be allowlisted and clearly disclosed.
8. A product must have at least one useful limitation or fit consideration, not only promotional copy.
9. Re-check volatile details before publication and set a review date.
10. If the evidence is incomplete, keep the record in draft.

## KPI dimensions
- Product card impressions.
- Product detail views.
- Affiliate redirect clicks by product, retailer, market and referring page.
- Network-confirmed conversions, commission, reversals and EPC.
- Product link freshness and broken-link rate.
- Click-to-order conversion from network reporting; site click events are not purchase events.
