# Trove & Tier — editorial operations and KPI plan

## Editorial operating principles
- Be useful before being decorative: every guide should help a reader make a decision or solve a real home problem.
- Distinguish manufacturer specifications, editorial research, independent testing and personal hands-on testing.
- Never invent product testing, reviews, ratings, availability, prices, retailer relationships, testimonials or expert endorsements.
- Recommendations should include dimensions, material, fit/compatibility, strengths, limitations, alternatives and market-specific retailer information where verifiable.
- Keep US, UK and Canada product availability and retailer links distinct.
- Disclose affiliate, sponsored and gifted relationships clearly.
- Date-check prices and stock; if not confirmed, direct readers to the retailer for current details.
- Keep content useful without affiliate links. Do not make a purchase the only solution to an organization problem.

## Core KPI framework
### Reach and acquisition
- Sessions by country, channel, landing page and campaign.
- Pinterest impressions, saves and outbound clicks.
- Facebook and Instagram reach, engagement and website clicks.
- Organic search impressions, clicks and landing pages.

### Reader usefulness
- Engaged sessions and engaged time.
- Article scroll depth / completion proxy.
- Search queries and zero-result searches.
- Category-to-guide navigation and return visits.

### Commercial outcomes
- Product recommendation views.
- Affiliate outbound clicks by product, retailer, market and source article.
- Network-reported orders, conversion rate, reversals and commissions.
- Revenue per 100 sessions and per affiliate click where sufficient data exists.
- Track click events separately from purchases; retailer/network reporting is authoritative for conversions.

### Partnership readiness
- Partnership page visits and qualified enquiries.
- Enquiry category, market, response time and outcome.
- Sponsored-content revenue and repeat partner rate.

### Trust and technical quality
- Product links past their verification date.
- Corrections and substantiated complaints.
- Core Web Vitals, Lighthouse and accessibility defects.
- Broken links, 404s, failed form submissions and service availability.

## Review cadence
- Weekly: content output, channel reach, search queries, outbound clicks, link freshness and technical errors.
- 30 days: establish baselines and identify the best room/topic/channel combinations.
- 60 days: improve formats and conversion paths based on evidence.
- 90 days: review affiliate performance, partnership readiness and content investment.

Do not set arbitrary follower or sales targets before baselines and traffic quality are known.
