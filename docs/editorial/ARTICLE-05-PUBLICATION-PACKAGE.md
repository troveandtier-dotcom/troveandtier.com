## 1. Editorial promise

A small bathroom is a wet room before it is a storage room. This guide helps readers make better decisions about moisture, available storage zones, plumbing access, electrical items, household chemicals and everyday reach. It is not a shopping list and does not claim that more storage is always the answer.

**Reader outcome:** a bathroom that is easier to use, easier to inspect and safer to maintain — without buying something simply to fill an empty wall.

## 7. Editorial and compliance guardrails

- Do not imply open baskets prevent mould or replace ventilation, leak repair or moisture control.
- Do not give universal fixing loads, shelf heights, electrical-code requirements or building-regulation advice.
- Keep UK, US and Canadian electrical guidance jurisdiction-specific.
- Do not suggest that child-resistant packaging is child-proof.
- Do not recommend decanting household chemicals into food or drink containers.
- Do not imply that a low under-sink cupboard is safe for chemicals around children or pets without appropriate security.
- No product testing, product endorsements, prices, availability or affiliate links are claimed.
